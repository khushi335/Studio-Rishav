My journey in visual storytelling began in 2015. Since then, I’ve worked across photography, filmmaking, editing, design and creative production, creating visual content for weddings, brands, events and commercial campaigns across Nepal and Australia.

My approach combines strong composition, authentic emotion and cinematic detail to create visuals that feel intentional, contemporary and memorable.
